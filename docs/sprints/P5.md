# P5: Email templates
Status: Implemented (owner phone check pending)
Goal: Students copy a ready, truthful email or cover letter for a specific role in under a minute.
Scope: `/templates`, "Draft email" on role pages, "Draft a follow-up" on applied tracker cards, Templates tab in Me; excludes AI drafting and sending mail.
Depends on: P2, P4

## Decisions
- Templates are rows in `email_templates` (public read, admin write), seeded from `supabase/seed/p5_templates.sql`. A database check rejects em dashes.
- Fill engine `lib/fill.ts` (pure, unit tested): `{{x}}` shows a visible `[x]` when unknown, `{{x?}}` drops the line. Em dashes are converted.
- Values only come from the user's profile, the chosen role, the tracker's applied date and `app_config`. Nothing is invented.
- Questions list asks only about unknowns or clashes: hours, allowance, start month, setup, exact-campus MOA.
- Projects: first two lines of the profile's projects only.
- The employer's subject format (from the role's apply instructions) replaces the default subject for application emails.
- Pre-send checklist: names right, resume attached (when needed), subject format, JobStreet cover letter reminder, no brackets left. Shows usable addresses to copy and bounced ones to avoid.
- Signed out: generic preview with markers and a sign-in prompt.
